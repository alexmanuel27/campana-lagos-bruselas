## Access and permission

No candidate is asserted to be permit-free. Public access to a park does not automatically authorize water removal, and regional park rules restrict entering the water. Request a written answer for manual collection of the planned small volume from a public path, with no entry into the water and no shoreline disturbance. Record the reply, contact person, and any constraints in the field log.

- City of Brussels: [Public Spaces and Green Spaces](https://www.bruxelles.be/departement-espaces-publics-et-verts) - `espacesverts@brucity.be`
- Brussels Environment (regional parks, L02): [contact](https://environnement.brussels/pro/contact) - `info@environnement.brussels`, 02 775 75 75
- Regional questions: [Brussels Environment contact](https://environnement.brussels/pro/contact)

## Why these five

They are geographically separated urban ponds with an identifiable municipal contact. No claim is made here about current trophic state, nutrient concentration, or legal exemption. The campaign may only describe a trophic contrast if independent, contemporaneous evidence supports it.
